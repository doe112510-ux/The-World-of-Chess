Famous Players

Since chess is such a popular sport, the best player become internationally famous. These chess players play in international tournaments like the Chess Olympiad, a biennial team competition that features 180 countries. The most accomplished chess players earn the title of Chess grandmaster. Some examples of people who have achieved this are Magnus Carlsen, Arjun Erigaisi, Wei Yi, Wesley So, etc. They are recognized as some of the best players in the world, and they earned their titles by playing for 8 to 10 years, preform exceptionally well at an FIDE tournament and earn a FIDE score of at least 2500.

How to become a grandmaster

Becoming a grandmaster is a long and difficult process. It is best to start at a young age to have more time to master the complexities and strategies of the game. To rise through the ranks of chess players world wide, you must play in hundreds of tournaments to increase your skills. One thing you must accept is that you are going to lose a lot, maybe thousands of games and dozens of tournaments, but these losses will teach you new skills that you can study and use to become a stronger player. Winning games will grant you FIDE score, which is the point system used by the World Chess Federation to rank it's players. The World Chess Federation is also know as Federation Internationale des Echecs, or FIDE for short. In order to officialy become a chess grandmaster, a player needs to have a FIDE score of 2500. You can earn score by playing in Norm tournaments. These are competitions has at least 3 grandmasters from all over the world, nine rounds and a control time of 120 minutes

Former grandmasters

The man in the picture is Anatoly Karpov, a Russian former chess grandmaster. He was the Chess world champion from 1975 to 1985, and was the highest rated player in the world for over 100 months, a feat that has only been bested by Magnus Carlsen and Garry Kasparov.
