How to Play

Chess involves a lot of strategy, which can be intimidating for people trying to learn. But the learning curve with chess is not as steep as some people think. While learning the more complex strategies to become a master can be difficult, if your just looking to play chess for fun with friends, all you really need to know is how to use the pieces properly, and you can form your own strategies, as every game of chess is unique and the moves of the players can force people to think in the context of that specific game.

It is important to know how to use the pieces together and how they function on their own. Both teams have 16 pieces: 8 pawns, 2 rooks, 2 knights, 2 bishops, a queen and a king.

The pawns are the weakest units on the board, they can only move one space in front of them, but two spaces if its their first movement of the game. They can attack diagonally and can only moving to the space in front of them. If they reach the end of the board, it will be traded for a higher value piece that the player has lost. This can be a bishop, knight, rook, or a queen.

Rooks are the pieces that look like mini castle towers. Each team has two rooks that start the game on the corners. They move in straight lines in any direction, and can move from one side of the board to the other as long as there are no pieces in it's way. Rooks can do a special move with their king called castling, where the rook and king can switch places. Learn more at strategies.

Knights are the pieces that look like horses. Their starting location is in between the rook and the bishop at both ends of the board. Knights have the most unique way of moving out of all the pieces. Knights move in an l shape over 3 spaces in any direction, but they can move either vertically two squares and horizontally one space, or one space vertically and two spaces horizontally. If the knight you want to move is on space 6c and you want to move it to 8d, you can move it up two and right one or up one and right two. Either way, it will end up at the same space. Another unique thing about knights is that they are the only piece in the game that can jump over another piece
