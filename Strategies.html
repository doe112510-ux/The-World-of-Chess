Strategies

Chess is a game of pure strategy, and there are hundreds of strategies you can use to gain an advantage against your opponent. It is important to know how to use the pieces together and how they function on their own. Both teams have 16 pieces: 8 pawns, 2 rooks, 2 knights, 2 bishops, a queen and a king.

The pawns are the weakest units on the board, they can only move one space in front of them, but two spaces if its their first movement of the game. They can attack diagonally
